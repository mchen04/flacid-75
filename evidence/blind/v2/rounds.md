# Blind cuteness rounds, version 2

Every round: Pip (hand-authored SVG from `components/Pip.tsx`, rendered by `scripts/blind-panels.tsx`) against one competitor.
Both panels are 400×400 on the same cream ground, named by a random id, order shuffled per pairing (`order.json`), judged by a fresh critic that sees only the two files.
The critic is told to judge cuteness only and never to identify a character or brand; a reply that names a brand voids that round.
The key for each round is in `panels/<round>/key.json`. Competitor sources are in `sources/sources.json`; they never ship in the app.
